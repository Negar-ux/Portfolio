Why accessibility, for me

I’m a Product Designer who brings together technical problem-solving and empathetic user research. My own experience with hearing loss shows me every day how small design decisions can open a door for someone, or quietly close it. It’s also why I value what users bring to the design process.

What I’ve worked on

My work is mission-driven, from early-stage startups to the nonprofit sector. I took an inclusive fitness app from concept to launch, and designed a website that brings together web accessibility and trauma-informed design. See my work

Research and teaching

At McMaster University, I co-authored three conference papers on equity-driven design in engineering education, and co-facilitated inclusive design workshops for engineering students. That work still shapes how I bring people into my design process today.
