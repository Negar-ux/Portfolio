Workshops & Research

Teaching and research on equity-driven design.

Workshops I’ve led

At McMaster University, I co-designed and co-facilitated three inclusive design thinking workshops for engineering students at the School of Engineering Practice and Technology. The workshops used equity-driven design approaches to help participants value diverse identities and bring equity principles into their engineering work. Together they reached 55 participants. Read the workshop case study

Research I’ve published

The workshops grew out of my work as a research assistant on inclusive learning through equity-based design in engineering education. Three papers came out of that work, all for the Canadian Engineering Education Association (CEEA) conference.

  • 2023 Deilami, N., Ngo, T.-A., Fleisig, R., Park, S., & Hemmerich, A. (2023). Inclusive Learning Through Equity-Driven Approaches to Design in Engineering Education. Proceedings of the Canadian Engineering Education Association (CEEA). Read the paper (opens in a new tab)
  • 2024 Hemmerich, A., Deilami, N., Ngo, T.-A., Park, S., & Fleisig, R. (2024). Fostering Inclusive Learning in Engineering Through Equity-Based Design Education. Paper presented at the Canadian Engineering Education Association (CEEA). Read the paper (opens in a new tab)
  • 2025 Hemmerich, A., Deilami, N., Ngo, T.-A., Satia, A., & Fleisig, R. (2025). Advancing Inclusivity in Engineering Education: Student perceptions of Design Thinking Courses and Workshops. Proceedings of the Canadian Engineering Education Association (CEEA). Read the paper (opens in a new tab)
